A little more about how I work

I appreciate a process that works, and I am usually the person asking how we can make it clearer.

I am at my best when the work calls for both careful judgment and practical problem-solving.

I ask thoughtful questions, identify the real issue, document what matters, and bring the right people together. I value clear communication, steady follow-through, and meetings that have a reason to exist.

When the process is unclear

I clarify the steps, define ownership, and help create a process people can follow.

When the decision is sensitive

I take the time to understand the facts, apply sound judgment, and keep the surrounding work organized.

When the system is not supporting the work

I translate the concern into a clear business need, and I work toward a practical solution.
